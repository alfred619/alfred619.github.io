About

A bit about me

I'm Alfred Shingai, a final-year BSc Applied Statistics student at the University of Zimbabwe and a data analyst who enjoys web development. I work across the analytics process — cleaning and transforming data with Python (Pandas, NumPy, SciPy), building interactive dashboards in Power BI with DAX and Power Query, and creating structured reports in Excel with Pivot Tables and XLOOKUP.

My latest product is StatLab Zim (Streamlit Cloud) — an open-source lab for descriptive stats, visualizations, and hypothesis testing. It handles messy real-world CSVs, shows assumptions and warnings, and exports results. I keep my practice lean: warm paper, strong typography, accessible markup, and 19 pytest tests before shipping.

Interests: data storytelling, Streamlit & modern web components, statistical literacy for non-technical audiences, and building small tools that ship.
Currently: seeking remote or in-office Data Analyst roles.
